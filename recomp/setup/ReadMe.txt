@NAME@ for Mac (Apple Silicon)

1. Drag @NAME@ to Applications.

2. Open it. The first time, macOS will stop it, because this app isn't signed with a paid Apple developer certificate.
   Click Done, then open System Settings > Privacy & Security, scroll down, and click Open Anyway next to @NAME@.
   You only have to do this once.

3. Drop your own copy of the game folder onto the app's window or its icon. The app checks it, builds the game for your
   Mac, and starts it. This takes a few minutes, and only happens once.

The app doesn't include the game. You need your own copy. Your files aren't changed.
